How your data is handled

Your question is sent to all four agents through the server, without sign-in.

Prompt agents: Foundry stores their conversations and responses.

Hosted agents: replay their browser-held history on each turn with store=False, rather than using a stored conversation for follow-ups. This is not a guarantee of zero service-side retention.

Starting a new comparison clears browser histories and continuation tokens. Neither this reset nor expiry of the demo’s 20-minute continuation token deletes Foundry-stored prompt conversations or responses.
